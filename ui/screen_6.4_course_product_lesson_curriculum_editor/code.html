Lesson Notes & Markdown Content

In this intensive session, we examine the physics of cylindrical lens glass and how horizontal compression crafts the iconic oval out-of-focus highlights. Remember that anamorphic distortion is not an optical defect—it is an intentional aesthetic narrative choice.

info Pro Tip: 1.33x vs 2.0x Squeeze Matching

When working with 16:9 modern sensors, a 1.33x squeeze factor delivers clean 2.39:1 scope without substantial sensor crop loss. For genuine vintage barrel distortion, pair 2.0x squeeze primes with a 4:3 open-gate sensor capture format.
